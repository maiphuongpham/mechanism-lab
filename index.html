01 — Complete information

The optimum is always a corner

Each good goes entirely to one country — never split. In the worked example the Home Ally wins both goods (revenue 240): its alliance package is worth more bundled than either half is worth alone.

02 — Private information

The revenue-regret frontier

No mechanism reaches first-best once values are private. A learned mechanism traces a frontier between the truthful VCG floor and the first-best ceiling — looser budgets buy revenue at the cost of regret.

03 — Three worlds

Bundles exactly when goods complement
